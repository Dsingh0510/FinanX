Before generating a suggestion, FinanX refreshes its tracked universe: up to 100 NSE stocks, 100 F&O contracts, 100 mutual funds, up to 50 listed bond/debt instruments and multiple bank FD entries. It then uses the available historical and live signals alongside your risk, goal, liquidity and horizon.

Live market signals can change quickly. FinanX uses them as one input; your risk, horizon and liquidity remain more important than short-term price movement.
